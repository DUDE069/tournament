Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

NPC's Esports Tournaments ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and age (to verify 18+ requirement).
  • Gaming Information: In-game usernames, player IDs, and team details.
  • Financial Information: Basic withdrawal details (e.g., UPI ID). Note: We do not store complete banking details, credit card numbers, or passwords. All payments are securely processed through UPI networks directly to the NPC Admin Account.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account securely.
  • To organize amateur student tournaments, verify participant identities, and distribute winnings.
  • To process micro-transactions and facilitate withdrawals.
  • To communicate with you regarding tournament match rooms or customer support.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Secure database and hosting providers (Firebase, Render).
  • Legal Authorities: Only if explicitly required by law enforcement or to protect our platform against cheating, fraud, or anti-money laundering (AML) violations.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard database security measures (Firestore secure rules) to protect your personal and financial data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023 (India)

In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Information: You have the right to know what personal data is being processed.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You can request the correction of inaccurate data or the deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent for non-essential data processing at any time by using the "Manage Data Preferences" link at the bottom of our website or by contacting our Grievance Officer. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

6. Data Fiduciary Identity

NPC's Esports Tournaments acts as the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act, determining the purpose and means of processing your personal data for tournament participation and prize distribution.

7. Grievance Redressal

If you have any grievances regarding the processing of your personal data, or if you wish to exercise your rights under the DPDP Act, please contact our Grievance Officer:

  • Email: supportnpcesports@gmail.com
  • Time to Resolve: We strive to resolve all complaints within the legally mandated timeframe.

8. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have general questions about your data or want to request account deletion, please contact us at supportnpcesports@gmail.com.
