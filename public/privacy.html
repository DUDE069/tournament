Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

NPC's Esports Tournaments ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy rights. This Privacy Policy details our data collection, processing, and storage practices under applicable laws including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act), 2023 of India.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Identifiers: Account nickname, email address, phone number, and verified age (strictly verifying 18+ requirement).
  • In-Game Telemetry: In-game usernames, player UIDs (BGMI / Free Fire), team rosters, and match statistics.
  • Financial Data: UPI ID provided strictly for processing withdrawal payouts. Note: We do not store complete bank account numbers, debit/credit card CVVs, or UPI PINs. All payments are verified directly through official UPI networks to the NPC Admin Account.
  • Technical & Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and push notification tokens (if permitted).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To securely authenticate your account and maintain tournament brackets.
  • To verify participant identities and enforce anti-cheat / fair play rules.
  • To process tournament registrations, prize pool allocations, and instant wallet withdrawals.
  • To broadcast match room details, schedule changes, and critical security notices.

3. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is shared exclusively with:

  • Infrastructure Providers: Encrypted hosting and cloud database infrastructure (Firebase / Google Cloud, Render).
  • Statutory Authorities: Disclosed only when strictly mandated by legal subpoenas, court orders, or law enforcement to investigate cheating, financial fraud, or AML violations.

4. Data Security Standards

We implement rigorous multi-layered security controls to protect player information:

  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All web communication uses industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols with modern cipher suites.
  • Database Access Matrices: Cloud Firestore security rules strictly isolate player documents, preventing unauthorized cross-user reads or tampering.
  • Zero Sensitive Financial Data: No payment passwords, PINs, or card credentials ever traverse or get stored on our servers.
  • No External Middlemen: All operations are handled solely through our verified portal and official admin channel.

5. Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023 (India)

In full compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you retain comprehensive rights over your personal data:

  • Right to Access Information: You may request a summary of personal data being processed by us.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You can request corrections to obsolete profile data or request complete account erasure.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may adjust or withdraw consent at any time via the "Manage Data Preferences" option or by emailing our Grievance Officer.

6. Data Fiduciary Identity

NPC's Esports Tournaments acts as the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act, determining the strict purpose and means of data processing for fair competitive tournament operations.
