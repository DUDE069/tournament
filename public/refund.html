Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: June 30, 2026

To ensure fair play and proper prize pool management, NPC's Esports Tournaments has a strict policy regarding refunds and tournament cancellations.

1. Registration vs Ongoing Stage Refunds

Registration Phase: If you register and pay for a tournament on the spot, but decide to cancel while the tournament is still in the Upcoming stage, you may request a refund. You must actively confirm your cancellation by stating, "Yes, I confirm that I want to cancel my slots and cancel my booking for this tournament" via our cancellation form.

Ongoing Phase: Once a tournament has officially started and moved to the Ongoing section, entry fees are strictly non-refundable.

2. 3rd-Party Access & Verifications

Refunds are only issued to verified NPC's Esports users. If a registration is flagged as 3rd-party access (i.e., the user details do not match a valid NPC user), the amount will not be refunded. We verify this by matching the Team Leader's Unique ID provided during the refund request against our official database.

3. Exceptions & Technical Errors

We will initiate a 100% refund of the entry fee under the following conditions:

  • Tournament Cancellation: If NPC's Esports Tournaments cancels a tournament for any reason.
  • Technical Errors (Inbox Verification Required): If your money is deducted and shows as verified, but you did not receive a confirmation message in your NPC Inbox, you may request a refund. Important: Our system guarantees an inbox notification upon successful verification. These notifications are retained for 48 hours and cannot be deleted. You must provide a screenshot of your inbox showing the missing/failed notification to claim this refund.

4. Processing Time & How to Request

Approved refunds are processed exceptionally fast, typically taking no more than 5 to 10 minutes on our end. However, depending on your bank and payment gateway (Razorpay), it may take 5-7 business days for the refunded amount to reflect in your original payment method.

To request a refund, use the "Cancel Registration" button on the Upcoming Matches tab. You will be asked to provide:

  • Your Backup Email
  • Your Backup Phone Number
  • Your confirmation agreeing to allow the support team 5 to 7 days to fully resolve your request if banking delays occur.

For emergencies or further help, please contact our support team at support@npcesports.com or +91 81339 07850 within 24 hours.
