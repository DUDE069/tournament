Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

To ensure fair play and proper prize pool management, NPC's Esports Tournaments has a strict policy regarding refunds and tournament cancellations.

1. Registration vs Ongoing Stage Refunds

Registration Phase: If you register and pay for a tournament, but decide to cancel while the tournament is still in the Upcoming stage, you may request a refund. You must actively confirm your cancellation by contacting our support team with your Team ID before the match starts.

Ongoing Phase: Once a tournament has officially started and moved to the Ongoing section, entry fees are strictly non-refundable under any circumstances. Prize pools are finalized based on registrations at this stage.

2. 3rd-Party Access & Verification

Refunds are only issued to the original UPI account that made the payment to the NPC Admin Account. If a registration is flagged as 3rd-party access (i.e., the user details do not match a valid NPC user), the amount will not be refunded. We verify this by matching the Team Leader's Unique ID provided during the refund request against our official database.

3. Exceptions & Technical Errors

We will initiate a 100% refund of the entry fee under the following conditions:

  • Tournament Cancellation: If the NPC Admin team cancels a tournament for any reason (e.g., lack of minimum slots filled).
  • Technical Errors: If your money is deducted and sent to the NPC Admin Account, but you did not receive a confirmation message in your NPC Inbox, you may request a refund by providing a screenshot of your successful UPI transaction.

4. KYC / AML Compliance

Because our platform operates on micro-transactions (typically under ₹50 entry fees), full KYC is not required for standard participation. However, we reserve the right to request Government ID (KYC) before approving any withdrawals if suspicious activity, cheating, or money laundering is suspected.

5. Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed exceptionally fast, typically taking no more than 5 to 10 minutes on our end. However, depending on UPI banking networks, it may take 3-5 business days for the refunded amount to reflect in your original bank account.

For emergencies or further help, please contact our support team within 24 hours of your transaction.
