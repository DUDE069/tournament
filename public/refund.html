Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

To ensure transparent prize distribution and fair play, NPC's Esports Tournaments enforces a structured policy regarding tournament entry fee refunds and match cancellations.

1. Registration vs Ongoing Stage Refunds

Registration / Upcoming Phase: If you register and pay for an event, but need to cancel while the tournament is still in the Upcoming stage (before match room credentials or bracket slots are finalized), you may request a refund. You must notify our support team with your registered Team ID prior to the match start window.

Ongoing / Live Phase: Once a tournament status transitions to Ongoing, entry fees are strictly non-refundable. At this point, slots and prize pools are locked for participating competitors.

2. Direct Source Verification

Refunds are remitted exclusively back to the original UPI account or ID that transferred the entry fee to the NPC Admin Account. To safeguard against financial fraud, refund claims are validated against the Team Leader's registered account profile.

3. Platform Exceptions & Technical Errors (100% Refund)

A full 100% refund of the paid entry fee is immediately initiated under the following conditions:

  • Admin Cancellation: If an event is cancelled by NPC Esports due to minimum slot thresholds not being achieved or technical game maintenance.
  • Payment Deducted without Registration Confirmation: If an entry fee was deducted from your bank and settled to the NPC Admin Account, but an unforeseen system error prevented your registration ticket from issuing, submitting your UPI UTR reference will yield an immediate resolution or refund.

4. Anti-Fraud & KYC Verification

While micro-transactions (under ₹500) do not necessitate full KYC for regular play, we reserve the right to request proof of identity before disbursing refunds if an account is flagged for cheating, multi-accounting, or suspicious payment origin.

5. Processing & Settlement Timelines

Once verified by the administrative team, refunds are approved within 5 to 10 minutes. UPI banking rails typically credit the refund to your source account within 1 to 3 banking days.

For urgent questions, contact our support team at supportnpcesports@gmail.com or via our official Instagram (@npc_universe.esp).
