About Us & Platform Overview

Official Platform Update • November 2026

1. What We Do

NPC's Esports Tournaments is a premier competitive gaming platform dedicated to hosting high-quality, skill-based mobile gaming tournaments. We provide a seamless, secure, and professional environment for teams and individual players to compete against each other for prestige and cash prize pools.

2. Our Core Services

  • Organized Tournaments: Daily and weekly scheduled matches with varying skill tiers for BGMI and Free Fire.
  • Fair Play & Anti-Cheat Enforcement: Strict moderation, device verification, and manual spectator reviews to ensure a level playing field without cheaters or unauthorized modifications.
  • Secure Prize Distribution: Integrated payment verification to seamlessly collect entry fees and quickly distribute winnings directly to winners' accounts via direct UPI transfers.
  • Live Leaderboards & Statistics: Real-time tracking of team performances, kills, and overall seasonal rankings.

3. Platform Security & Fair Play

We hold security and fair competition to the highest standards across all tournament tiers:

  • Verified Identification: In-game player nicknames and game UIDs are rigorously verified against official team rosters prior to match room issuance.
  • Direct UPI Processing: All entry fees and withdrawals are securely routed through our official NPC Admin Account. We never store personal banking passwords, card CVVs, or UPI PINs.
  • No Third-Party Brokers: NPC Esports operates strictly without unauthorized agents or external intermediaries. Never transfer money to unofficial accounts.
  • DPDP Act (India) Compliance: Player account information and telemetry data are safely encrypted and managed under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

4. Transparent Pricing Structure

Our pricing is completely transparent. The entry fee for each specific tournament is clearly displayed on the tournament card before registration. Entry fees typically range from ₹10 to ₹500 per team depending on the total prize pool and tournament scale.

There are zero hidden subscription fees or recurring charges. You only pay the exact entry fee when you choose to register for a specific tournament.

5. Our Mission

We aim to empower the grassroots esports community by providing players with a robust, reliable, and secure platform where they can monetize their gaming skills competitively and build their esports career.
